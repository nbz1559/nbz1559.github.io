FAQ

Can I use Kestrel in GTA Online?

No. Kestrel is for story mode only. Using mods in GTA Online can get your account banned.

How do I install it?

Download the launcher, open it, and click Install. We handle everything from there.

Is it free?

Yes. Kestrel is 100% free and always has been and always will be.

Will Kestrel get me banned?

As long as you use the mod for its intended purpose (Story-mode only), this risk of getting banned is little to none.

Is Kestrel Multi-Platform?

No, at the moment it only supports Windows 10 and 11.
